M1lash Studio — Privacy Policy

M1lash Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel M1lash (@m1lash93) to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to read analytics of that channel.

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, through Google OAuth (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API). It does not access other users' data, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) are stored in the owner's GitHub repository for reports. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
