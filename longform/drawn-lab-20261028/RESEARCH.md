## Long film fact sources

- Chemical energy, ions inside / electrons outside, reversible charge: US DOE https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsbatteries and Appendix B https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2023-09/KORE_PowerEA_Sept%202023%20Appendices.pdf (battery basics p44). Geometry is illustrative, not a complete cell model.
- Brightness / low signal / background / update aftermath: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120745 and Google https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/6090612?hl=en; no universal hours or savings percentage.
- OLED self-emitting vs LCD backlight: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-gb/109039. Dark-theme effect depends on technology/brightness/content; no invented benchmark.
- Background scheduling: Android Developers https://developer.android.com/training/monitoring-device-state/doze-standby. There are maintenance windows and exceptions; no claim all work stops.
- Closing apps: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph83bfec492/27/ios/27 and https://support.apple.com/en-tm/109359. Claim specifically scoped to iPhone, not every possible app/OS.
- Capacity, peak delivery, age, low charge and cold: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-euro/101575. Model-specific cycle targets deliberately omitted.
- Thermal state, workload and restrictions: Google https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15679416?hl=en; Apple https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph3006fbee4/27/ios/27; Samsung https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/galaxy-battery/charging-tips/.
- Compatible USB-C charging: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-us/105099. Adapter rating is not a claim of constant device draw. No unsafe modification or battery disassembly instructions.
- Charging stages / cumulative full cycle: Apple https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/charge-the-battery-iph63eecc618/27/ios/27. The 50+50=100 example explains the definition, not model lifespan.
- Overnight optimization / limits and occasional full charge: Apple https://support.apple.com/en-us/108055 and Google https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/6090612?hl=en. Feature availability varies; 80% is not a universal magic threshold.
- Wireless charging alignment / cases / temperature: Google https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/12816122?hl=en and Samsung charging tips above. No unsupported quantitative efficiency comparison.
- Adaptive Battery / temporary post-update work: Google https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7015477?hl=en. Battery Saver tradeoffs: https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/6187458?hl=en.

Power/energy arithmetic and the journal's 20/10/5/3/2 units are explicitly educational examples. Reservoir, city and couriers are analogies. No vendor UI, real device test, diagnosis, guaranteed recovery or performance forecast is fabricated.
